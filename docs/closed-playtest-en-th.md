# Guardians of Galia — Small EN/TH Desktop Playtest

This is an observed test for 5–10 people. The website does not collect feedback or personal information. Players tell the owner directly what they thought.

## Mix of players

- 2–4 Thai-first players
- 2–4 English-first players
- at least 2 players using a lower-powered laptop
- a mix of players who regularly play games and players who do not

## Give players only this instruction

> Choose any faction. Play the mode that looks most interesting, then try two other modes. Say out loud whenever you are unsure what to do.

Do not explain controls or objectives unless the player is completely stuck. The point is to see whether the website teaches itself.

## Watch for

- Can the player switch EN/TH and choose a faction without help?
- Which mode do they choose first, and why?
- Do they understand the win condition before moving or shooting?
- Can they explain what they earned after a run?
- Do they know where permanent upgrades live?
- Does any image, text, modal, or navigation item shift, overlap, or sit below the fold?
- Does keyboard or mouse input ever feel delayed?
- Does the player voluntarily start another run?

## Direct questions after 15–20 minutes

1. Which mode was the most fun?
2. Which mode was the least clear?
3. Was anything too easy or unfair?
4. What reward or unlock did you want next?
5. Did any Thai or English sentence sound strange?
6. If you had five more minutes, what would you play?

Record only anonymous notes unless the player explicitly asks to be identified. Improve the three issues mentioned or observed most often before adding another large feature.
